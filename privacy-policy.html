Privacy Policy

Mutation Lab: Idle Evolution
Package name: com.mutationlab.idleevolution
Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mutation Lab: Idle Evolution handles information when you play the game.

Short version: the game does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

Mutation Lab: Idle Evolution does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages.

Local Game Data

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device. This may include your DNA amount, unlocked mutations, creature progression, mission progress, upgrade state, and the last saved offline time used to calculate offline earnings.

This local data is used only to run core game features such as saving progress, unlocking mutations, showing collection progress, missions, and offline rewards.

Offline Progress

Offline progress is calculated from locally stored game state and a locally stored timestamp. This information is not sent to the developer or to a server.

Ads, Analytics, and Purchases

The current version of Mutation Lab: Idle Evolution does not include third-party advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or rewarded ads. If these features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before they are used.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Because the game does not intentionally collect personal data, there is no personal data to share.

Children's Privacy

Mutation Lab: Idle Evolution is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress is stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the game.

Security

We use a local-only save approach for game progress. No method of digital storage is perfect, but the game is designed to avoid collecting personal data in the first place.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Mutation Lab: Idle Evolution, please contact the developer using the contact email listed on the game's Google Play Store page.
